# Home e páginas públicas do Hub Animal Joinville

Scope: every public route (home first; serviços, agenda, organizações, detalhes, sobre, privacidade inherit). Mode: persuade on the home, read on the inner pages. Audience: adopters and people who need help for an animal, equally. Read-only site, strict CSP, no JS required, pt-BR, WCAG 2.2 AA. Approved comp: .impeccable/mocks/decision/assigned.png (option 1, chosen by the user).

## Direction contract

THESIS: Joinville's Festa das Flores exhibition: every service and fair is an entry with a tied paper tag stating source, date and contact. Refuses the category's white page with rounded cards, paw icons and stock photos.

OWN-WORLD: hydrangea blue-violet ground (#3b46a8 family) across the whole page; cream aged-paper tags with twine and a punched eyelet; red-magenta rubber-stamp dates; orchid magenta (#c2187a) only for the one action; leaf green; deep violet ink (#17123d); warm wedge serif for headings. One large gouache botanical illustration per section, never a mosaic. No cards or boxes: entries separate by their tag and by space.

STORY: the visitor sees a dog and a cat among hydrangeas, understands this is Joinville's place for animal help, searches or jumps to rescue or abuse report, and sees the next fairs as stamped tags.

FIRST VIEWPORT: mark and name top-left, nav top-right; dog and cat gouache plate across the top half; question "De que ajuda seu animal precisa?" centered below; search inside a large tied tag with a magenta "Buscar"; two underlined urgent links; "Próximas feiras" begins at the fold.

FORM: Cidade das Flores, assigned candidate 5 of the grounded list; seed: none (unavailable).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
